DOCUMENT COPILOT + OFFERS ASSISTANT : DEMO CHEAT SHEET
=======================================================
All data is fictional (Rahul Sharma, PAN ABCPE1234F). Documents are valid for "today" = 31 Oct 2026:
required months are Jul, Aug, Sep 2026 (October is accepted but never required).
If you run the demo on another date, run:  python scripts/make_demo_docs.py   (it regenerates the files for that date).

URL: http://localhost:8501


=====================================================================================================
PART A: THE FAST PATH (no uploads, about 2 minutes)
=====================================================================================================
Consent page -> open "Demo mode: try a sample applicant" -> click one of the three profiles.

  Profile       Click this button           Details form (already pre-filled)             Story
  ------------  --------------------------  --------------------------------------------  ---------------------------------
  STRONG        "Strong profile"            400000 / 36 months / EMIs 5000 / score 780    Premium + most offers open
  BORDERLINE    "Borderline profile"        400000 / 36 months / EMIs 8000 / score 720    Premium is NOT eligible (best demo)
  LOW INCOME    "Low-income profile"        50000 / 12 months / EMIs 0 / score unknown    Only the small-ticket offer opens

Then click "Show my offers".

Things to do on the offers page (borderline profile):
  1. Open "Why / what's needed" on Premium Salaried Loan -> income 52,400 vs 60,000 (7,600 short), score 720 vs 750.
  2. Open "Try different numbers": drag the amount slider, watch statuses change, press "Apply these values".
     The chat adds a note that the offers were recalculated.
  3. Sort by "Lowest EMI" / "Highest amount".
  4. Open "What the assistant can see" under the chat: the exact masked data the AI reads.
  5. Click "Select this offer" on Standard Personal Loan -> identity check (demo OTP: any 6 digits, e.g. 123456)
     -> after the selfie step, open "Lender handoff package" (contains the selected offer + eligibility run).

Upload each file into the matching box on the checklist:

  Box on the page                File to upload                              Expected result
  -----------------------------  ------------------------------------------  ------------------------------------------
  PAN Card                       01_pan_card.jpg                             Fields auto-filled, PAN shown as ABCPE****F
  Aadhaar Card (front)           02_aadhaar_front.jpg                        Aadhaar shown masked (XXXXXXXX0124)
  Aadhaar Card (back)            03_aadhaar_back.jpg                         Address + pincode 560001
  Salary Slip (3 files at once)  04_salary_slip_2026-07.jpg                  3 slips verified, months Jul-Sep 2026
                                 04_salary_slip_2026-08.jpg
                                 04_salary_slip_2026-09.jpg
  Bank Statement                 05_bank_statement_OK.pdf                    Period 01/07/2026 to 30/09/2026 verified

Then: "Review my details" -> tick "I confirm" -> "Save and continue" -> "See my loan offers".
At "A few more details" type:  amount 400000 | tenure 36 | EMIs 8000 | pick "Type my score" 720 -> "Show my offers".

--- Failure scenarios (do these BEFORE the happy path, in a fresh application) ---
  1. PAN box   <- X1_pan_card_BLURRY.jpg           REJECTED locally: "the photo is blurry" + how to fix (no AI cost)
  2. PAN box   <- X2_pan_card_TOO_DARK.jpg         REJECTED locally: "too dark"
  3. PAN box   <- X3_pan_card_LOW_RESOLUTION.jpg   REJECTED locally: "resolution too low"
  4. PAN box   <- 02_aadhaar_front.jpg             "This looks like an Aadhaar card (front), but we asked for your PAN card"
  5. Bank box  <- X4_bank_statement_WRONG_PERIOD.pdf   "covers Apr-Jun 2026, but we need Jul-Sep 2026"
  6. Bank box  <- X5_bank_statement_IFSC_TYPO.pdf      IFSC shown as invalid; type HDFC0001234 in the form -> re-verified.
                                                       (Type a wrong value 3 times to show "sent to manual review".)
  7. Salary box <- 04_salary_slip_2026-07.jpg + 04_salary_slip_2026-09.jpg + X6_salary_slip_2026-10_current_month.jpg
                                                       "Salary slip for August 2026 is missing."  (Remove the Oct slip,
                                                       upload 04_salary_slip_2026-08.jpg -> fixed.)
  8. Salary box <- 04_salary_slip_2026-07/08/09 + X6 ...  October (current month) is accepted, never required.

Privacy moments:
  - After "Save and continue": "The photos and scans you uploaded have been deleted".
  - Sidebar -> Privacy -> tick -> "Delete my documents and data" (removes files, fields, offers and chat).


=====================================================================================================
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG DURING THE DEMO
=====================================================================================================
  - Blank fields after an upload usually mean the AI service was busy: click "Replace this document" and upload again,
    or just type the values in the form shown (the correction path is part of the demo).
  - Chat says "Answered from fixed rules": the AI was unavailable or its answer failed the number check; the facts are
    still exact. That is the safety net working.
  - Use PART A (demo mode) if the document services are slow: it needs no document AI at all.
